## Hi, I'm Reem
I'm interested in what happens when code starts interacting with the real world.

I'm studying Mechatronics Engineering at Canadian University Dubai, where mechanical systems, electronics, and programming come together. What interests me most is the point where these things stop being separate pieces and start working as one system.

I've always found the connection between hardware and software particularly interesting. A program can look perfectly fine on a screen, but what happens when it has to read a sensor, respond to a changing environment, or control something physical? That's the kind of question that draws me toward embedded systems, robotics, and industrial automation.

I enjoy understanding how things work beneath the surface, from the logic that controls a system to the small details that determine whether it behaves as expected. I'm especially interested in the relationship between sensing, decision-making, and movement, and how relatively simple components can come together to produce something much more complex.

Most of what I'm learning involves experimenting, troubleshooting, and figuring out why something doesn't behave the way I expected it to. Sometimes the interesting part isn't getting something to work, but understanding why it didn't work in the first place.

I'm still exploring where I want to take all of this, but I'm drawn to systems that interact with the physical world and make it a little more intelligent, precise, or efficient.

**The part that interests me most is the connection between what we tell a machine to do and what it actually does.**
